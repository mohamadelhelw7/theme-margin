# Changelog

## 1.0.0
- First release of Margin, a theme for calm casual wear brands. It's set like a printed catalogue, with white pages, black type, wide margins and numbered plates with italic captions.
- New sections: Margin header (side rail or top bar), Plate hero, Chapters, Letters and Margin footer.
- Product page: a catalogue page. Numbered photo plates with captions sit beside a buying column that stays in place as shoppers scroll. On phones the plates swipe.
- Catalogue plate product cards. Theme settings → Catalogue sets the caption style, the word before plate numbers and the side rail width.
- Five presets: Margin, Linen, Graphite, Studio and Sage.
- Clothing drawings for products without photos, in four drawing colors you can change.
- Included by default:
  - Header: moving announcement bar, a mega menu (panels beside the side rail) and the language selector.
  - Homepage: a hero that takes a video, one or two images and adapts to the image or a chosen ratio, plus email signup, reviews, customer support and customer videos.
  - Product page: star rating, accordion rows, stock status, upsell, delivery timeline, reviews, size guide, and FAQs at the end.
  - Footer: payment icons and language selector.
  - Cart: a cart upsell and an optional free shipping bar, in the drawer and on the cart page.
- Full Arabic with right-to-left layout, using Vazirmatn.
